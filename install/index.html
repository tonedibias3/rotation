Rotation

Add Rotation to your Home Screen

Rotation runs from your Home Screen like any other app. It takes about 20 seconds.

Open Rotation

iPhone and iPad

  1. 1

    Open the link in Safari

    Use Safari, not Instagram or another in-app browser. If you tapped the link in a bio or message, tap the compass or the three dots and choose Open in Safari.

  2. 2

    Tap the Share button

    It is the square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom of the screen (or the top on iPad).

  3. 3

    Tap Add to Home Screen

    Scroll down the share menu until you see it. If you do not see it, tap Edit Actions and add it.

  4. 4

    Tap Add

    Keep the name Rotation and tap Add in the top right. The icon now sits on your Home Screen.

  5. 5

    Open Rotation from your Home Screen

    Always open it from the icon, not from Safari, so your closet stays in one place.

Android

  1. 1

    Open the link in Chrome

    Tap the link, then open it in Chrome if it opened somewhere else.

  2. 2

    Tap the three dots

    They are in the top right corner of Chrome.

  3. 3

    Tap Add to Home screen or Install app

    Then tap Install or Add to confirm.

  4. 4

    Open Rotation from your Home Screen

    Use the icon from now on.

Your closet lives on your device. There is no account. Always open Rotation from the same Home Screen icon, and save a backup to iCloud or Files from Settings now and then so you can restore it on a new phone.
