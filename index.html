What it does

Log the fit

Pick the pieces you wore, add a photo, tag the occasion and time of day, and rate the look. Plan ahead on the calendar and log up to two outfits a day.

Shoe and scent of the day

Keep a shoe rotation and a fragrance rotation next to your clothes. Share just your shoe or just your scent, whether or not you log a full outfit.

Share cards made to post

Turn an outfit, a single piece or your monthly recap into an image for Instagram or X. Choose light or dark, square or story, and show names, brands, both or neither.

Know your closet

See what you wear most, which pieces and shoes have been resting, and what each piece costs per wear. Prices and where you bought something are never shown on a share card.

Private by default

No account, no sign-up. Your closet and photos stay on your device, and you can save a backup to iCloud or Files and load it anywhere.

Questions

What is Rotation?

Rotation is a closet and outfit log app. You add your clothes, shoes and fragrances, log what you wear each day, and see which pieces you actually wear, which ones are resting and what each piece costs per wear.

Is Rotation free?

Rotation is free during the beta. Pricing will be announced before it launches in the App Store and Google Play.

How do I get beta access?

Follow @rotation.fit on Instagram and @rotationfit on X, then send a DM saying you want in. You will get the link and a short note on how to get started.

Do I need an account?

No. There are no accounts and no sign-up. Your closet, outfits and photos are stored on your own device.

Where is my data stored, and can I back it up?

Your data stays on your device. In Settings you can save a backup file to iCloud or Files and load it on another device, so you can move your closet to a new phone.

Is Rotation on the App Store and Google Play?

Not yet. It runs in your phone's browser today and can be added to your home screen. App Store and Google Play versions are coming soon.

Can I log fragrances and sneakers, not just clothes?

Yes. Rotation has sections for tops, bottoms, shoes, outerwear, accessories and scents. You can set a shoe of the day and a scent of the day, and share either one on its own.

What can I share?

You can turn an outfit, your shoe of the day, your scent of the day, a single piece or a monthly recap into an image for Instagram, X or anywhere else. Choose light or dark, square or story, and show names, brands, both or neither. Prices and where you bought something are never shown.

What does cost per wear mean?

Cost per wear is what you paid for a piece divided by the number of times you have worn it. Rotation works it out for you once you add a price and log your outfits.
