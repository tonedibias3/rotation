Rotation privacy policy

Last updated October 13, 2026

What Rotation stores

Your closet, outfits, photos, notes and settings are stored only on your own device. Rotation does not have accounts and does not send this information to us or to anyone else.

Photos and camera

Rotation asks for camera and photo access only so you can add pictures of your clothes and outfits and save outfit images to your library. Those pictures stay on your device.

Optional photo search

If you turn on photo search in Settings with your own Google search key, the words you search for are sent to Google to find product pictures. Nothing else is sent.

Weather and location

The weather chip is optional. If you allow location, Rotation uses it only to show the weather. Your approximate position (rounded to about a mile) is stored on your device and sent to a free weather service (Open-Meteo) and a place-name service (BigDataCloud) to get the forecast and the town name. It is not tied to you or kept by us. You can type a city instead.

Sharing

When you tap Share or Save, the image goes where you choose. Prices and where you bought an item are never included.

Tracking and ads

Rotation has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Deleting your data

Delete items or outfits inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything stored on your device.
